## Content overview

ClaudyGod is a worship-music and ministry streaming app. All catalogue content
(audio, video, live sessions, Word of the Day, announcements) is **published by
ClaudyGod administrators** through the admin console. There is **no
user-generated content visible to other users** — the only user inputs are:

- support-request messages (private, seen only by staff),
- an optional private rating comment (seen only by staff),
- live-session chat comments/suggestions, which are **moderated** (`visibility:
  'visible' | 'hidden'`, admin-controlled) and attributed by display name.

No profanity, violence, sexual content, gambling, or drug references in the
first-party catalogue. External links open the ministry website, YouTube, or
WhatsApp support.
